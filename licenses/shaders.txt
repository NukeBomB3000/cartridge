omega-zero's screen looks (SELECT+X in a game) are, but for the scanlines and the CRT, slot's ports
of shaders from the libretro shader collection
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders
by way of slot, https://github.com/BrandonKowalski/slot

LCD by Gigaherz (LCD3x). Public domain.

Dot by Themaister. Public domain.
Changes in slot: sharper dots (falloff 4.0) and a 0.85 blend.

Grid, the line weighting of zfast_lcd_standard by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), copyright (C) 2017.
GNU General Public License version 2 or later.

Simpletex, simpletex_lcd by jdgleaver, its grid the line weighting of zfast_lcd_standard as
above. GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
Changes in omega-zero: its preset's parameters fixed, and the paper texture drawn as grain
rather than read from textured_paper.png.

The paper's grain is Dave Hoskins' "Hash without Sine", MIT License, copyright (c) 2014.

The scanlines and the CRT are omega-zero's own, as are Transparent and DMG. Transparent is
zfast_lcd by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), GNU General Public License version 2 or later, under
omega-zero's own code after the effect of mattakins' Pixel Transparency (bright pixels letting
the unlit LCD's backing through, the dark ones casting a shadow on it), without its code. DMG is
after the Game Boy's four-green palette. Both share Grid's line weighting.

omega-zero itself is GNU General Public License version 3 or later, which these licenses permit.
